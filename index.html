一、招生对象与报名条件 Eligibility
• 年满18周岁（部分专科项目放宽至23周岁），持有效外国护照的非中国籍公民；Aged 18+ (some vocational programs up to 23), non-Chinese citizen with a valid foreign passport;
• 具有高中毕业或同等学力（本科/专科申请），身心健康，无犯罪记录；High school graduate or equivalent (for UG/vocational), good health, no criminal record;
• 遵守中国法律法规与学校规章制度，尊重中国社会风俗和公序良俗。Compliance with Chinese laws, university regulations and social customs.
二、报名时间 Application Timeline
• 秋季入学：一般为前一年11月至当年4月报名，各校起止略有差异；Fall intake: usually Nov (previous year) to Apr; dates vary by university;
• 春季入学：一般为当年9月至11月报名；Spring intake: usually Sep to Nov;
• 奖学金申请截止普遍早于普通申请1-2个月，其中中国政府奖学金（CSC）渠道多在1-4月受理。Scholarship deadlines are usually 1-2 months earlier; CSC channels mostly Jan-Apr.
三、报名方式与流程 How to Apply
• 线上申请：在学校国际学生在线申请系统注册账号、填写报名信息；Register and apply via the university's online admission system;
• 提交材料：护照、最高学历成绩单、毕业证（或预毕业证明）、语言成绩、个人陈述、推荐信、经济担保证明、《外国人体格检查表》等；Documents: passport, transcripts, diploma, language scores, personal statement, recommendation letters, financial guarantee, physical examination form;
• 缴纳报名费：200-800元不等，缴费后上传缴费凭证；Pay the application fee (RMB 200-800) and upload the receipt;
• 材料审核：学校对申请材料进行评审，部分院校或专业安排面试。Document review; some universities/programs arrange interviews.
四、语言与学业成绩要求 Language & Academic Requirements
• HSK参考：专科/本科中文授课项目一般要求HSK4级（180分以上）起，部分文科专业要求HSK5级；研究生项目多要求HSK5级（210分以上）；HSK reference: HSK4 (180+) for vocational/UG Chinese-taught programs, HSK5 (210+) for most postgraduate programs;
• CSCA参考：越来越多高校将CSCA学业水平考试作为本科录取与奖学金评定依据，工科类常要求数学+物理达到B以上；CSCA reference: increasingly used for UG admission and scholarship assessment; Math+Physics B or above is common for engineering;
• 英文授课项目：通常免HSK，需提供IELTS 6.0+ 或 TOEFL 75-80+ 等英语成绩。English-taught programs: HSK-free; IELTS 6.0+ or TOEFL 75-80+ required.
五、学费与相关费用 Tuition & Fees
• 学费：专科约1.2万-2.2万元/年，本科约1.5万-5万元/年，中外合作办学项目较高；Tuition: RMB 12,000-22,000/yr (vocational), RMB 15,000-50,000/yr (UG); higher for Sino-foreign programs;
• 住宿费：约400-1,500元/月；医疗保险：约800元/年；教材费、居留许可等按实际发生缴纳。Housing: RMB 400-1,500/month; insurance: ~RMB 800/yr; other fees charged as incurred.
六、奖学金 Scholarships
• 主要类型：中国政府奖学金（CSC）、省/市政府奖学金、校长/新生奖学金、国际中文教师奖学金及专项奖学金；Main types: CSC, provincial/municipal, presidential/freshman, Intl Chinese Teachers and special scholarships;
• 资助力度：优秀者可获学费全免+生活费补助；同一份材料通常可同时申报多类奖学金。Outstanding applicants may receive full tuition + stipend; one set of materials often fits multiple applications.
七、审核录取与签证 Review, Admission & Visa
• 流程：材料评审（部分面试）- 发放《录取通知书》和JW202表 - 办理X1/X2学习签证 - 入学注册并购买医疗保险。Process: review (plus interview for some) -> admission letter + JW202 form -> X1/X2 visa -> registration + insurance.
八、其他通用条款 Other Common Clauses
• 遵守学籍与考勤管理，出勤率不达标可能影响考试资格与签证延期；Attendance is managed; poor attendance may affect exams and visa extension;
• 经学校同意，国际学生可依法参加勤工助学和校外实习；Work-study and internships are allowed with university consent;
• 转专业、转学等事项须按学校有关规定提出申请。Major/school transfers follow university regulations.

HSK 与 CSCA 的关系How HSK and CSCA Relate

HSK 是国际中文水平标准化考试，证明申请人具备在中文授课项目中学习与生活所需的中文能力；CSCA 是来华留学本科入学学业水平测试，评估申请人的学科基础与学术潜力。两者定位不同：HSK 决定语言门槛，CSCA 提供学术评估，不少院校会同时参考，具体要求以目标院校招生简章为准。HSK is China''s standardized Chinese proficiency test, certifying the language ability needed for Chinese-taught programs. CSCA is the China Scholastic Competency Assessment for undergraduate admission, evaluating academic readiness and potential. HSK sets the language threshold; CSCA provides the academic assessment. Many universities consider both — always check the target university''s admission brochure.

HSK 中文水平考试HSK Chinese Proficiency Test

考试简介HSK 是面向母语非汉语者的国际中文水平标准化考试，由汉考国际（CTI）组织实施，现行 HSK 3.0 标准共 1—9 级，成绩用于来华留学、就业与能力认证。AboutHSK is China's standardized Chinese test for non-native speakers, administered by Chinese Testing International (CTI); HSK 3.0 covers levels 1–9.
报考要求无国籍、学历硬性门槛，按自身水平选择级别即可报考；中文授课本科项目通常要求 HSK 4—5 级，部分顶尖院校或专业要求更高。RequirementNo hard eligibility threshold — choose a level matching your ability. Chinese-taught undergraduate programs usually require HSK level 4–5.
报考条件持有效护照报名；考试当天须携带与报名信息一致的身份证件；未成年人报名需监护人同意。ConditionsValid passport required; bring matching ID on test day.
报考时间HSK 1—6 级 2026 年每月一场（1—12 月共 11 场）；HSK 7—9 级每年 5 月、11 月各一场，均为机网考。DatesLevels 1–6: one session per month in 2026; levels 7–9: May and November (computer-based only).
报考方式在中文考试服务网（chinesetest.cn）注册账号，选择考点与场次完成在线报名；也可通过所在学校或考点集体报名。How to applyRegister online at chinesetest.cn, or apply through a test center.
缴费方式报名时在官网在线缴费（支持在线支付方式），缴费成功才算报名完成；费用按报考级别与考点收取，具体金额以报名页面显示为准，逾期未缴费将取消考位。PaymentPay online during registration; the amount depends on level and test center, and unpaid registrations are cancelled.
成绩与证书机网考成绩一般考后约 1 个月公布，纸笔考试稍晚；可在官网查询并下载电子成绩报告。ResultsResults are published about one month after the test; download the score report online.

CSCA 来华留学本科入学学业水平测试CSCA China Scholastic Competency Assessment

考试简介CSCA 全称 China Scholastic Competency Assessment，由中国国家留学基金管理委员会（CSC）组织、联合中国高校专家共同研发，用于评估国际学生申请本科阶段的学科基础与语言能力，成绩被多所高校纳入本科申请评估。AboutCSCA is developed by the China Scholarship Council with Chinese universities to assess applicants' academic foundation and language ability for undergraduate study.
报考要求面向拟申请中国高校本科的国际学生；不同院校、专业对是否认可 CSCA 及成绩要求不同，申请前请核对目标院校招生简章。RequirementIntended for international applicants to Chinese undergraduate programs; acceptance and scores vary by university.
报考条件持有效护照的非中国籍考生，按官网要求如实填写个人信息并上传证件材料；考试科目按报考院校与专业要求选择。ConditionsNon-Chinese citizens with a valid passport; choose subjects according to your target program.
报考时间2026 年 11 月—2027 年 6 月共 6 场：11 月 14—15 日、12 月 19—20 日、2027 年 1 月 23—24 日、3 月 13—14 日、4 月 24—25 日、6 月 26—27 日。首场报名窗口为 2026 年 10 月 15 日 15:00 至 10 月 21 日 23:59（北京时间）。DatesSix sessions from Nov 2026 to Jun 2027; first registration window 15 Oct 15:00 – 21 Oct 23:59 (Beijing time).
报考方式官方唯一报名门户为 csca.cn：注册登录 → 了解考试 → 选择考试场次 → 填写考生资料与科目 → 在线缴费 → 按时参加考试（居家网考或集中机考）。How to applyRegister only at csca.cn: sign up, choose a session and subjects, pay online, then sit the test online at home or at a test center.
缴费方式在 csca.cn 报名流程中在线缴费，缴费完成方视为报名成功；费用标准以报名页面显示为准。PaymentPay online within the csca.cn registration flow; the exact fee is shown there.
成绩使用考后可在官网查询并下载成绩报告，用于向院校提交申请；成绩有效期与认可范围以各校最新简章为准。ResultsDownload your score report online and submit it with your university applications.

奖学金体系总览Scholarship System Overview

🎯 中国政府奖学金（CSC）
教育部设立。全额：免学费+住宿+生活费+医保；部分：单项资助。每年1—4月申报。Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC): Full — tuition + dorm + stipend + insurance; Partial — single items. Apply Jan–Apr.
🏛️ 省/市政府奖学金
如北京、上海市政府奖学金、浙江省奖学金、广东省来华留学生奖学金、厦门陈嘉庚奖学金等，由高校组织申报。Provincial & Municipal Scholarships — e.g. Beijing, Shanghai, Zhejiang, Guangdong, Tan Kah Kee (Xiamen). Applied via universities.
🏫 高校校长/新生奖学金
依据学业成绩、CSCA/HSK成绩与面试表现评定，分一等（学费全免+补助）、二等（半免）等档。University President / Freshman Scholarships — merit-based; Tier-1 full tuition + stipend, Tier-2 half tuition.
🤝 国际中文教师奖学金
面向中文及相关专业学生，HSK3起可申请一学期进修，HSK4起可申请学历项目。International Chinese Language Teachers Scholarship — HSK3 for one-semester study, HSK4 for degree programs.
🌏 专项奖学金
"一带一路"专项、企业冠名、东盟/非洲专项等，覆盖学费或生活津贴，关注各校当年简章。Special Scholarships — Belt & Road, corporate-funded, ASEAN/Africa programs. Check annual brochures.
📝 CSCA 学业水平考试
中国留学服务中心组织的国际学生学业水平考试，部分高校将其作为录取与奖学金评定依据。CSCA Test — organized by CSCSE; used by some universities for admission and scholarship assessment.
